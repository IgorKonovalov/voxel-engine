### The amendment (2026-11-21): the no-face verdict on the stream workers

- **Built:** `bake/seal.rs`: each worker reduces the chunk it loads (its `ChunkMasks`) to a
  shell (whether a face lies between two of its own voxels, and its six 64 x 64 border layers'
  opaque and solid bits, a uniform layer held as its class), inserts it in one shared map behind
  a mutex (`Shells`, owned by `FacePrep`), and under the same lock reads its own verdict and its
  present neighbours' again; each verdict carries the map's sequence number. A neighbour with no
  shell reads open (air). `bake/queue/sealed.rs`: the queue takes the verdicts with the masks
  (`take_prepared`), the later sequence number holding, and a sealed chunk is no candidate
  (`candidacy`), passed and settled; an edit's box forgets the edited chunks' shells and clears
  the verdicts within a voxel at a new sequence number (an edit before the first result is held
  pending and forgotten at the handle's arrival); the queue prunes verdicts and shells of chunks
  the world does not hold once they outnumber twice the mirror's chunks plus 8 192. Lines: the
  `bake queue:` line's `sealed by the workers N (shells, B)`, the census line's `no face N (M at
  the residency's edge)` and a second census line with the verdict's worker cost, the
  autopilot's masks line with the verdict's mean and max. `FacePrep::new` replaces the struct
  literal in `apps/flythrough/src/setup.rs`.
