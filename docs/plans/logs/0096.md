**Phase 10b's three facts, read before any code** (`gpu_world/columns.rs`, `gpu_world/
lights.rs`, `gpu_world/slot.rs`, `bake/pipeline.rs`, `shaders/bake.wgsl`, `vxe-world/src/mips.rs`):

- **(i) false as worded.** A chunk's sheet is set at its upload: from the full chunk's bricks,
  or from its level-1 mips for an LOD chunk (`note_columns`). A mip cell is solid when any of
  its eight children is (`majority`: the most frequent non-air material, air only when all are
  air), and its top is the 2-voxel cell's upper voxel (`sheet_of`, `top = (z + 1) * cell - 1`).
  So a chunk that arrives full over its LOD (the camera coming near) replaces the mip sheet and
  can lower a cell's top, by a voxel at least, more where the majority's material changes
  opacity. Every upload is a mirror change, and `relist_zeroed` reads every change's column
  within the light radius, so the lowering is read; but "the lowest top never falls when a
  chunk arrives" does not hold. A first arrival into a column with no sheet over the cell, or
  below its top, does not lower it.
- **(ii) holds.** `emitters_near` reads the full chunks' light lists (`lights.chunks`, noted
  only for full records, dropped at a swap to LOD) within `boxes_within(c, coord, r)`;
  `job_table` takes the placed ranges of the same lists within `light_radius * 64` voxels of
  the job's box, the same gap; an LOD chunk's emitters are in neither.
- **(iii) holds.** An arriving chunk is an upload event in `take_changes`; its column lies
  within the light radius of a ruled chunk whose reach it enters, so `relist_zeroed` reads the
  rule again there, and `note_lights` registers its emitters in the same sync.
