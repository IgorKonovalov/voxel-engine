  takes it to 0.11 at twice the lag; the far end is the bake's at any cap). The clamp leaves a
  sample alone where `K m >= 1`, the mean `m` near `p`: `K >= 1 / 0.3 = 3.3` keeps every rim
  pixel (p at least 0.3) unclamped; `FIREFLY_K` 4, the smallest integer above it.
