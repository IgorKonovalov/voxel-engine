- **`SKY_HISTORY_CAP` and `FIREFLY_K` (done-when 2), ADR-0070's arithmetic from the hit rates
  above:** a running mean of `N` frames at hit rate `p` has a relative standard deviation
  `sqrt((1 - p) / (p N))`. Rim, p 0.34 to 0.38, N 64: `sqrt(0.62 / (0.38 x 64)) = 0.160` to
  `sqrt(0.66 / (0.34 x 64)) = 0.174`; N 128: 0.113 to 0.123. Far end, p 0.0095 to 0.018,
  N 64: 1.27 to 0.92. `SKY_HISTORY_CAP` 64 (the rim's spread 0.16 to 0.17; doubling the cap
  takes it to 0.11 at twice the lag; the far end is the bake's at any cap). The clamp leaves a
  sample alone where `K m >= 1`, the mean `m` near `p`: `K >= 1 / 0.3 = 3.3` keeps every rim
  pixel (p at least 0.3) unclamped; `FIREFLY_K` 4, the smallest integer above it.

## Phase 3: History across a stair in the far-normal regime

- **Mechanism as built:** `stair_w` is the far normal's weight of a world cube-face hit in the
  face normal mode; over one half the history test compares `far_unmixed` normals (the
  smoothed normal recovered from the stored blend, both at this hit's weight) within
  `HISTORY_COS` and drops the material test. The key and sky rays start at `sky_origin`, the
  shadow origin plus `ray_lift = n LIFT w` (LIFT 1.0) where `w` is over one half, and
  `skip_origin_cell` makes `march` step one voxel past a solid first cell. A reading hook
  (`LightHooks::no_stair`) turns both off.
