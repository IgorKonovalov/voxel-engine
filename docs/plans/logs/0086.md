## Phase 4: The sky visibility baked per brick direction

- **Mechanism as built:** `BRICK_WORDS` 7 to 9: words 7 and 8 hold six `u8` visibilities
  (direction `f` at byte `f % 4` of word `7 + f / 4`) and `VIS_BAKED` (bit 16 of word 8).
  The bake job's face entry draws a sky ray at each sample below `sky_n`
  (`ceil(SKY_BAKE_RAYS / faces drawn of the direction)`, at most the face's samples;
  `SKY_BAKE_RAYS` 256), cosine-weighted about the face normal from the sample's point, through
  `shadowed_styled_walk` with the frame's coarse mode and shadow styles (`BakeRun::sky`, the
  job uniform's word 4), capped at `light_radius * 64` voxels, below the horizon blocked, in
  dimensions 1002 and 1003. A direction's visibility is the mean of its faces' open fractions.
  Summaries and parents average the visibility over the records that carry it; a dug brick's
  provisional record fills a missing direction's visibility with the mean of its others;
  `brick_lit` (ring 3) also reads a visibility over zero. `E_sky` is a pass after the sky
  view (`sky_irradiance.wgsl`): 32 x 32 strata of equal cosine measure per axis, the LUT read
  through `sky_view_radiance` (moved out of `sky_radiance`), into a 96 B storage buffer
  (`Frame::sky_irradiance`), which the temporal pass binds at 16. The temporal pass adds
  `v E_sky(dir) / h(dir)` (`h` 1 for `+z`, a half sideways; `baked_sky_term`) to a face's
  stored part where the face's own cell holds a visibility, the visibility bilinear as the
  emissive value, `E / h` mixed by `n_a^2` for a shading normal off the face's; the light pass
  binds the store at 4 and does not add its sky ray's sample where `sky_baked` holds (the ray
  is still cast).

## Phase 5: The rays' own long history, and the sky ray only where stale

- **Mechanism as built:** the light pass casts the sky rays where the hit's face holds no fresh
  baked visibility (`sky_baked`: no store cell, no VIS_BAKED, or `SKY_STALE`, bit 17 of word 8)
  and writes their open share to a new `rgba8unorm` target on the lighting grid
  (`Frame::visibility_samples`: r the share, a 1 where cast); the direct target no longer
  receives any sky ray. The temporal pass keeps the visibility pair (`Frame::visibility_pair`,
  two `rgba16float`: r long mean, g 0, b short mean, a age), reprojected with the direct part's
  taps, reset where the direct part resets and on LIGHTING_SKY_CHANGED (`pair_step`). Cap
  `sky.sky_history` (the old `_pad` word of the sky uniform; `Lighting::sky_history`, default
  64, `--sky-history`); SKY_SHORT 8; FIREFLY_K 4 times `max(long, short)`, applied when the
  age before any fall is over 8; band `3 sqrt(r (1 - r) / 8) + 0.02`; out of the band the age
  falls to `max(2, floor(8 band / |b - r|))`. Where no ray is cast at a face with a baked
  visibility the pair is seeded with it at age `sky_history`. The sky term `v E(n)`
  (`sky_open_by_normal`, the Phase 4 `E / h` mixed by `n^2`) goes to a cube face's stored
  part, any other hit's direct sample before its blend. A dig's ring bricks are marked
  `SKY_STALE` in `take_boxes` (store copy and pool, `Store::mark_sky_stale`); the re-bake's
  records clear it; `fill_directions` keeps it. `--sky-rays 0` parses (bake only; the stale
  value is used where the ray would run). New hooks `LightHooks::sky_ray_always`, `no_clamp`.
  `FRAME_PASSES` gained `sky irradiance` (Phase 4's fourth sky pass shifted every timestamp
  name by one: Phase 4 bar 7's per-pass names read one pass late, its frame total stands).
  `encode.rs`'s filter and output binds moved to `frame/output_binds.rs` (1199 to 1058 lines).
