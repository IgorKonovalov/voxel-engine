### The edit-box reset, read (not fixed: Phase 2 owns it)

`crates/vxe-render/src/marcher.rs` (the cache clear's dispatch, before the gradient pass) and `cache_clear.wgsl`:

- The dispatch is `[sum of dilated box volumes / 64, 1]` in one dimension. At 65 535 groups the cap is 4 194 240
  dilated voxels a frame: one box of side `n` voxels (dilated `n + 2`) reaches it at `n` = 160 (162^3 = 4 251 528).
- Each box's count is a `u32` product of three `(max - min + 3)` sides, and the sum over boxes is `u32`; one side of
  1624 or more (1626^3 > 2^32) wraps it. The shader's `size.x * size.y * size.z` is `u32` too.
- With more than `MAX_EDITS` (64) boxes, `Frame::invalidate` sets the history's `overflow` (history bit 0 off, the
  screen flushed) but keeps only the first 64 boxes, which are all the clear sees: the later boxes' cells keep their
  counts.

## Phase 2: The table is the only emissive store, and the default

### Conditions

Tree: the Phase 2 code at d940f32 (`wip(render)`), main merged at the merge after it (f107977's amendment), done-when

### What was built (deviations marked)

- The cell is ten words: key (two words: each coordinate's low 20 bits, the face, a valid bit in word 1), count, three
  Q17.15 sums, kept emitter (two words), kept luminance, frame stamp (the last four written by nothing yet).
  `Lighting::{table_cells, table_cap, table_read}`; `DEFAULT_TABLE_CELLS` 2^20. A claim exchanges word 1 then stores
  word 0 (a race can share a slot between two keys with equal word 1 and a word 0 of 0).
- **Deviation:** the counters (misses this frame, occupied) live in a 16 B buffer of their own (`CacheStats`), not in
  the table's header, so `frame_bytes` counts the table at exactly 41 943 040 B; the stats buffer is not counted.
- **Deviation:** the miss counter counts lighting pixels whose key found no slot, not faces;
  `a_full_table_counts_its_misses` asserts it against the pixels and prints the faces.
- **Deviation:** an edit frame whose dilated boxes have more faces than the table has cells (6 x voxels > cells), or a

### What was built (deviations marked)

- The light pass offers each lit sample's emitter face (its voxel `floor(x - 0.5 n)` and face) to the cell
  (`cache_offer`). **Deviation:** the kept luminance is the centre-to-centre irradiance from the cell's face centre to
  the emitter face's centre with the emitter's emission now and one shadow ray (`kept_irradiance`), not the sample's
  contribution, so the gradient pass's re-shade of an unchanged scene reads the same value; the shadow ray is cast only
  when the unshadowed value beats the kept one.
- **Deviation:** no compare-exchange on the point. Word 8 packs the luminance's high 16 f32 bits with a 16-bit tag of
  the kept key; `atomicMax` on it, then the key's two words stored by the winner. The guard reads a key whose tag
  differs from the packed word's as a torn store: it re-packs the key with its value now, or reopens the cell when
  that key lights nothing.
- The gradient pass re-shades the stratum pixel's cell's kept emitter and reopens the cell (count, sums, kept key and
  luminance cleared, `cache_stats.reopens` counted) beyond the dead zone 0.05 of the larger. **Deviation:** the fresh
  sample of a cell lit by nothing runs only for a capped cell whose sums are 0 (an uncapped cell still draws samples
  in the light pass; a first attempt without the cap condition reset dark faces with few samples every frame, and
  `a_face_reads_its_mean_from_the_first_sample` failed on it).
- **Deviation:** the gradient pass runs in every shaded view (its lamp lambda only with the gradient history bit), with
  a group 2 of the table and its counters at light.wgsl's bindings 3 and 4.
- **Deviation (public API):** `Frame::read_table_reopens`, the reopen count since the table was last cleared
  (`CacheStats` word 2); `TableStats` is unchanged (its literal in `hud.rs`'s tests).
- Tests in `settle.rs` (done-whens 1 to 3) on a box of their own (`guard_box`): an ore wall at x = 3, the one-voxel

### Part 2b: the preload restructured (02883f7, wip; deviations marked)

- **The sweep:** a lane per voxel, eight 64-lane workgroups a brick (a slab of 8 x 8 x 1 voxels each); a face with no
  cell goes to a probe list instead of being probed in the sweep's lane.
- **Deviation (state per slab, not a cell):** each slab carries a stamp word (`PRELOAD_STAMPS`, the bricks within 16 of
  the eye at their coordinates modulo 33, eight a brick, 1 149 984 B): the world's epoch, a brick tag and a state
  (probed, done). The CPU raises the epoch on every encode with an edit box, a changed `bytes_written`, a table clear, the
  table's chunk sweep, or a C_PRE or radius change. A face no emitter reaches is probed once an epoch; a slab whose faces
  hold no cell the sweep wants is skipped on later visits. No cell is claimed for an unlit face; the claim rule is
  unchanged.
- **Deviation:** a face whose probes were all dark, or refused for the load or a miss, is not probed again in that
  epoch (before, it was probed again on every pass).
- **The probes:** eight faces a 64-lane workgroup, a lane a sample, summed in workgroup memory; a claimed cell is offered
  every lit sample's emitter (the cell keeps the brightest, as `cache_offer`'s max). This replaces the deviation "offered
  the brightest probe sample's emitter only" (the same result).
- **Deviation (where the fill and the probes run):** in the light pass's dispatch (`shade_preload`, extra workgroup rows
  after the lighting grid, indirect), so their samples run beside the pixels. Every decision that reads a cell's count or
  bit runs in two dispatches before it, in the `preload` slot, which `FRAME_PASSES` now orders before `light`: the args
  pass (one 64-lane workgroup) and the sweep. A fill entry carries its draws left (from the count the sweep read, or
  fixed for a probe's new cell); a cell whose entry ran out or that a probe claimed goes to a list whose
  `CACHE_PRELOADING` bits the next args pass applies. With the fill and the probes reading counts beside the pixels
  (the first form), `the_lighting_targets_are_scale_free` read 2/255 at 24 and 19 pixels; with the ownership split it
  reads 1/255, 0 pixels past.
- **Deviation:** the probes' claim rule reads the last frame's misses and occupancy (`pre_last_misses`,
  `pre_last_occupied`, taken by the args pass before the light pass; the CPU no longer clears the misses with the
  preload on), as the plan's wording has it ("the previous frame counted no miss").
- **Deviation (the trickle):** gone. While still with both lists empty, the sweep visits B bricks a frame until a whole
  pass over the offsets at this epoch finds every slab done, then none until the camera moves, a list fills or the epoch
  changes. `PreloadUniform::trickle` is removed.
- **Deviation:** the sweep lists a cell under C_PRE only if it holds a kept emitter (a lit sample was offered) or fewer
  samples than a probe draws (`preload_wants`): a light-pass cell of a face in view no emitter reaches is left to the
  light pass. A test of the sums instead read 2/255 in the scale-free test (the quantized sums of a dim face round to 0
  at a scale of 1/64).
- **Deviation:** the cut's tie-break and the fill's random numbers hash the cell's key, not its slot.
- The plain light pipeline (`shade`) is built on its first use: with both it and `shade_preload` built at
  `Frame::new`, `castle_gate_golden`'s peak RSS read 1 126 150 144 B against its 1 GiB bar; lazily, 930 742 272 B.
- `PreloadStats` gains `probed`, `probe_drops`, `done` (slabs skipped) and `probes`; `scanned` counts slabs.
- `frame_bytes`: 163 488 336 B at the default (+ 1 936 504 B on ca2b0e9: the stamps, the probe lists, the left and
  claimed lists, the fill lists' second word), asserted in `upscale.rs` and `look.rs`.
- `preload_level_readings` gains `VXE_PRELOAD_SIZE` (WxH) and `VXE_PRELOAD_COST_ONLY` (no reference or pixel readback,
  half-resolution lighting: the Budgets' frame). The reference (done-whens 14 and 16) now walks the run's cameras at the
  largest radius with C_PRE at C_CAP, holds every 16th camera and the last until the lists read empty, and keeps cells of
  at least 1024 samples; entering pixels within 64 voxels whose face has none are counted (`no reference`).
- Goldens re-blessed on lavapipe (old in `live/golden-old-p4i/`): `dark_cave.png` mean 0.196, p99 2, max 8, over 2
  0.20 %; `dark_cave_half.png` mean 0.503, p99 4, max 18, over 2 4.78 %, over 8 0.14 %.

### What was built (deviations marked)

- `probe_update.wgsl`: the rays shade a hit with `albedo / PI x PI lamp_lit` alone (no emissive sample, hit emission or
  earlier bounce; the emission-scale rows go unread); the blend is the running mean `age / (age + 1)` only (`HYSTERESIS`,
  `YOUNG_UPDATES`, `YOUNG_HYSTERESIS`, `LAMP_MOVED_AGE` gone), h 0 and age 1 for a restarted entry (an edit touched it, it
