(Y and Cg near 250 in red, step 0.125; Co near 400 in blue, step 0.25): FSR 2's depth-clip
pass stores the prepared colour as YCoCg in rgba16float, unclamped (pre-exposed, up to 988 here),
and `G = Y + Cg`, `B = Y - Co - Cg` (`R = Y + Co - Cg` for blue) cancel two quantised values of
the bright channel's size. With the other channels exactly 0 (the plain rig) Y and Cg round
alike and cancel exactly; with any small other light they round apart, and the residual
follows the bright channel's value, so it draws contours of the light's falloff (rings round
the light, a cross where the falloff's iso-lines meet the face's axes). A white light has
Co = Cg near 0, so the bright value rounds in Y alone and every channel shares it. The native
path (no YCoCg storage) holds within 1/255.

**The fix changes the upscaler's colour handling, so it goes to the orchestrator; 5b stops here
and no code changes.** Options:
- (a, recommended) **FSR 2's prepared colour in rgb, not YCoCg**: `depth_clip.wgsl` stores the
  prepared rgb (still rgba16float, the depth clip in a), and `accumulate.wgsl`'s
  `load_prepared_input_color` converts to YCoCg in f32 on load. Each channel then rounds alone
  (relative 2^-11 of itself), no channel carries another's error; the format, size and bytes
  are unchanged; two lines. The Lanczos sum and the clip box are linear in the colour, so YCoCg
  of the sum is the sum of YCoCg.
- (b) the prepared target in rgba32float: removes the rounding (f32's residual at 988 is about
  6e-5) at 8 B a render pixel more (a format change and a `frame_bytes` move).
- (c) store YCoCg of the tone-mapped colour: does not help; the inverse tone map multiplies
  the residual back by `1 + max`.
