## Phase 1: Where the frame's CPU goes

- **Names in the tree.** The stages are `STEP_STAGES` in `bake.rs` (setup, filter, boxes,
  withdraw, digs, candidates, census, sort, checks, list, refresh, poll, collect, place,
  submit, fades), summed per step into `QueueStats::stages` (`StepStages`: each stage's ms, the
  step's own ms from the frame's call, `opened`). `setup` is `Frame::bake_queue_step`'s work
  before `BakeQueue::step` (the light hold, the parameters, the bind group), so the call's
  start is passed in (`marcher.rs`, one line). The per-second line is a second line, `bake
  queue step:` (regime, samples a baked chunk, +chunks, jobs, coarse, bake ms/s, opened of
  steps, step p50 open / not, `reach-bound N of M in view waiting`, each stage's mean ms a
  step); the `frames:` summary gains `frames: queue step <A|B|mixed|idle|pooled>:` lines
  (steps, p50, p99, opened share, open and not p50, each stage's p50/p99 and open/not p50).
  The regime is `Regime::of(samples, chunks)` (`REGIME_B_MAX` 3e5, `REGIME_A_MIN` 6e5; a
  second with samples and no chunk baked reads A, one with no sample `idle`); the tally is
  `StepTally`. The reach-bound count is the census's: in-view waiting candidates whose
  distance in chunks plus the light radius exceeds `QueueView::full_radius` (the streamer's
  `r_full`; `None`, a bounded world, counts none). The HUD's queue row ends `step X ms` (the
  last line's step p50). The window takes `--adapter` (`ShellConfig::adapter`,
  `vxe_app::pick_adapter`, over the adapters that present to the surface).

## Phase 4: No black for missing light (exploration)

- **Names in the tree** (ADR-0053). The summary is one record (`BRICK_WORDS`) at
  `bake::SUMMARY_AT`, word 512 of the block, just past the directory: `layout` returns the
  directory and the summary's place (`BLOCK_HEAD_WORDS`), so every block size (`Store::alloc`,
  `place`, `job_words`, `stats.bytes`) takes the 7 words with no other change. This departs
  from the resume note's "`BRICK_WORDS` before the block's first word", which would have moved
  every caller of a block's first word; the table word still reaches the summary with no second
  read (`block + 512`). `block_summary` and `mean_record` (`RecordMean`: a direction's mean over
  the records holding it, the material most of them keep, the lowest on a tie, the mask's
  union); `LightSummaries` (no GPU: the summaries as shown and the two parent levels, `set`
  returns the two parent records to write, `chain` the CPU's walk); `ParentTable` (after the
  chunk table, level 1 then 2, cells world-aligned `c >> l`, `(size + 2^l - 1) >> l` plus one
  a side). `Store::put` and `Store::show` write the summary into the block's words and the
  parents follow; `remove` empties it; `rewindow` rewrites the whole parent table with the
  chunk table (same size), a reset empties it. The mode is `MissingLight` in `STORE_FLAGS` bits
  2 and 3 (`FLAG_MISSING_SHIFT`), kept across a pool rebuild (`Store::keep_missing_light`).
  The counters are words 3 and 4 of the frame's store-counter buffer (20 bytes;
  `StoreStats::fallback`, `still_black`), not the pool's header: the store binding is
  read-only in `temporal.wgsl`. `temporal.wgsl`'s `store_fallback` runs only where
  `store_brick_value` has no weight: own summary, the six face neighbours' mean, level 1, level
  2, 0. A first block under a fallback mode fades over `FADE` from `fallback_block` (the
  block's layout and tails, each cell's directions the chain's value; the CPU chain at emission
  scale 1, while the GPU's multiplies each source's material scale: they agree at scale 1).
  The step's new stage `summaries` (17th) takes the summary and parent time out of `place`,
  `fades` and `filter`, in that order. `--missing-light black|neighbour|parent|chain`, key `7`,
  banner `missing light <mode>`, the HUD store row `missing N (light <mode>: fallback F black
  B)`, the per-second line `no value / fallback / still black / coarse` shares and the store's,
  summaries' and parents' bytes. Default `black`.
