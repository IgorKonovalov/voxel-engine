## Phase 1: Where the frame's CPU goes

- **Names in the tree.** The stages are `STEP_STAGES` in `bake.rs` (setup, filter, boxes,
  withdraw, digs, candidates, census, sort, checks, list, refresh, poll, collect, place,
  submit, fades), summed per step into `QueueStats::stages` (`StepStages`: each stage's ms, the
  step's own ms from the frame's call, `opened`). `setup` is `Frame::bake_queue_step`'s work
  before `BakeQueue::step` (the light hold, the parameters, the bind group), so the call's
  start is passed in (`marcher.rs`, one line). The per-second line is a second line, `bake
  queue step:` (regime, samples a baked chunk, +chunks, jobs, coarse, bake ms/s, opened of
  steps, step p50 open / not, `reach-bound N of M in view waiting`, each stage's mean ms a
  step); the `frames:` summary gains `frames: queue step <A|B|mixed|idle|pooled>:` lines
  (steps, p50, p99, opened share, open and not p50, each stage's p50/p99 and open/not p50).
  The regime is `Regime::of(samples, chunks)` (`REGIME_B_MAX` 3e5, `REGIME_A_MIN` 6e5; a
  second with samples and no chunk baked reads A, one with no sample `idle`); the tally is
  `StepTally`. The reach-bound count is the census's: in-view waiting candidates whose
  distance in chunks plus the light radius exceeds `QueueView::full_radius` (the streamer's
  `r_full`; `None`, a bounded world, counts none). The HUD's queue row ends `step X ms` (the
  last line's step p50). The window takes `--adapter` (`ShellConfig::adapter`,
  `vxe_app::pick_adapter`, over the adapters that present to the surface).
