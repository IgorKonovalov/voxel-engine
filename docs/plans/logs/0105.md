### The trace's radiance source (reading-only, with the Phase 1 commit)

`HeldPart` gains `radiance_from` (`RadianceFrom::OldRecord`: the kept brick's old record held
the direction; `Filled`: it held other directions, their mean as `fill_directions` takes it;
`Neighbours`: the brick held no record, the mean of the records around it) and
