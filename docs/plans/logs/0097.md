## Phase 3a: Highlight protection

- **The mechanism as built:** the composite's meter tile's last word (0 before) carries the
  largest `clamp(log2(max(max(r, g, b), 2^-16)), -16, 16)` over the tile's hit pixels (sky
  pixels -16); the exposure pass bins the tiles' maxima by pixel count into 128 quarter-EV bins
  over [-16, 16) (a workgroup histogram of atomics), reads the level over which `1 - P/100` of
  the metered pixels lie (linear in its bin), smooths it with `step_capped` (the eye's step and
  rate; a reset or the first metered frame jumps), and clamps `current` under `white / 2^level`
  besides the ceiling; `white` 2.863 (the ACES fit's inverse at 0.95; 0.95 under the identity).
  The state is a 16-byte buffer of its own (`ExposureHighlight`, binding 4), read back in the
  ring after the 32-byte state; `ExposureState` is unchanged, `ExposureUniform` 112 -> 128 B.
