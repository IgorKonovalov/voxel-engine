## Phase 2: class A, the rest

Machine: the dev reference (RTX 3080 Laptop host); GPU runs on lavapipe (the tests' forced
fallback adapter). Four lanes active: `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=4`, scopes capped at 6G.

- **The condition variables (8d9fe04d):** `Shared::wake` split into `arrived` (waited under the
  inbox's lock, notified by `send`) and `room` (waited under the outbox's lock, notified by
  `take` and `take_until`); `halt` notifies `arrived` under the inbox's lock and `room` under
  the outbox's. `bake::thread` and `split_tests::thread` by filter: 20 run, 20 passed.
- **`at_cap` cleared by a take:** `take` and `take_until` set `at_cap = false` as they empty the
  outbox (a put at the cap then finds room). Without it a `wait_step` right after a take
  returned on the stale flag before the waiting put had run.
- **Read first, the four unsure rows and the two `gpu_loading` rows:**
  - `the_cycle_parts_sum_to_the_time_between_two_steps` (`cycle.rs`): left. Each of its 12
    frames waits (`frame_stepped(k)`) for step k before the next send, so no message
    coalesces and frame k's take holds step k - 1's entries whole; the cycles counted are the
    steps' own, so `>= 8 of 12` is structural. Its 1 s `within` guard is Phase 3's.
  - `sky_empty_noon_golden`, `sky_empty_sunset_and_night_goldens`: the count can move. The
    exposure ring copies a slot at each encode while one is idle and requests the earlier
    slots' maps at the next encode (`exposure.rs` 812, 871); `readout` polls once without a
    wait (835) and returns the newest slot mapped by then, or `None` (then the test's `expect`
    panics). Remedy: `device.poll(wait_indefinitely)` before `exposure_readout` in
    `Empty::zenith_exposure`.
  - `the_sky_term_switches_without_a_dip` (`dig_run`): the count can move. The lockstep
    issuer's `collect` (`bake/issue.rs` 167) takes a read-back early when its map flag is
    set, which a device poll sets, and waits only past the depth; the frames before
    `DIG_AT - 1` had no device wait (the shots' blocking reads start there). Remedy: a device
    wait after each frame's `f.step` in `dig_run_with`. The rig is shared by the soak tests
    `a_dig_leaves_no_dots` and `a_reset_pixel_starts_from_its_record`.
  - `a_source_switch_shows_the_loading_frames_before_the_new_world`: the count can move. A
    loading frame runs steps until `ms_since(t0) < slice_ms` fails (`run/loading.rs` 957), 25
    ms by default, and names the stage it ends in; a stage whose steps all fall inside one
    slice is never named, and the frame count is wall over 25 ms. Remedy: the run gets
    `--load-slice 0.05`, the form `a_sliced_pre_roll_bakes_what_an_unsliced_one_bakes`
    already uses (one step a loading frame).
  - `a_sliced_pre_roll_bakes_what_an_unsliced_one_bakes`: the count can move, and not by a
    change in its test file. **Stop for the architect, test unchanged.** (1) `PreRoll::decide`
    boxes the coarse pass at `work_ms - near_ms > box_ms` (5000 ms, `COARSE_PRE_ROLL_MS`) and
    caps at `cap_ms` (120 s) of summed wall step time (`bake/pre_roll.rs` 193, 202), so a slow
    run of either arm ends with fewer steps. (2) Both arms are lockstep (headless), whose
    `collect` takes a read-back early when it has mapped; the on arm's loading frames each
    wait on the device before stepping (`run/loading.rs` 943), the off arm (`load_all`, 443)
    steps back to back with no wait, so which step reads a read-back differs by GPU pace. The
    test compares `(steps, chunks, samples)` of the two arms for equality. Its file can set
    only flags; no flag waits the off arm's steps or turns the box off.
