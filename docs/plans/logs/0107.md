## Phase 4: the sizes and the emitters from the image

**Scope (restated):** selection sizes a new full record from its held image; the backstop
takes the image's counts under the write lock once the four conditions pass; `upload_record`
takes the image's emissive flag and light lists instead of the rescan set and `note_lights`.

**What was built (the tree at this phase's commit):**

- `image.rs`: `image_need` (selection's read: field off and the key matching, no lock);
  `usable_image` checks without taking (a refused image is dropped and counted, a usable one
  stays held); `take_image` takes it and counts `images_used` once the backstop passed, so a
  deferred chunk keeps its image.
- `window.rs`: `select` reads the flags at its start when unwritten (the first sync sized
  under all-clear flags before, and the image's key could not match there); a chunk with no

  also computed inline and asserted equal.
- `slot.rs`: `upload_record(image: Option<&mut SlotImage>)`: `emits` from the image, no
  rescan inserts, the lists moved out of the image into `take_lights` (with

  `emitter_lists` (doc-hidden).
