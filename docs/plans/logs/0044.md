1. **defect.** `crates/vxe-audio/src/mixer.rs:482` (`retire`), also `:487` and `:501`. If the
   retire channel and the hold are both full, or the `Recycler` is gone, the callback drops
   what it retires, and that can include a voice's `VoiceBuffers`. The callback then frees
   memory, and the pool loses that voice for good. Reproduced on a scratch crate (not
   committed):

     game thread recycling between blocks.

   keeps the source only up to `MAX_VOICES` held items.

   reserve them a hold of their own, or put the hold's limit on buffer-less items only. On

## Fix pass (side-lane review findings 1, 2, 3)

### Finding 1: retire never drops a voice's buffers

- Mechanism (`mixer.rs` module comment): retired voices wait in a voice hold of `MAX_VOICES`
  slots that cannot overflow (64 buffer sets, each in one place), flushed before a separate
  source hold of `MAX_VOICES`; a dropped `Play` past the source hold still drops its source on
  the callback (a free only if it carried the last reference). With the `Recycler` dropped,
  buffers go to a `dirty` store on the mixer and are cleared when a `Play` takes them.
- `tests/mixer.rs::a_burst_past_every_hold_keeps_every_voice_buffer_and_frees_nothing` (64
  looping voices, then 200 `Play`s and 64 `Stop`s in one block, 4 recycle-and-render rounds,
  64 new `Play`s): on the old `mixer.rs` it fails with 128 frees on the callback (64 voices x
  2 buffers); with the fix 0 frees and 64 voices.
- `tests/mixer.rs::a_dropped_recycler_keeps_every_voice_buffer_on_the_mixer`: old code, round
  2 starts 0 voices of 64; fixed, 64.
- `tests/output_hash.rs::a_fixed_scene_renders_to_a_pinned_hash` (900 blocks, HRTF, default

## Phase 3: the frame builds its bind groups once per size

- **`BindCache`** (`gpu_util.rs`): a bind group is kept with its layout, binding numbers and
  the textures and buffers it binds (clones of the `wgpu` handles, compared by identity); a
  pass that binds the same resources takes the group back, and a group and its views are
  created only on a miss. `tick()` at each encode drops what the last 2 encodes did not take,
  so a group per history parity survives the frame that does not bind it, and a replaced
  resource is held at most 2 encodes; `set_sizes` clears it. `bind_io` and `buffer_entry` are
  gone (no user left).
- Users: every group of `encode.rs` (the world group, the hit, resume, downsample, light,
  temporal, variance, a-trous, composite, gradient, upscale, sharpen, probe and gather groups),
  FSR 2's ten (`FrameInputs::binds`, the frame's cache), and the exposure pass (a cache of its
  own; `Frame::bind_groups_built` and `views_built` sum both). The present pass keeps its last
  source's group (`Presenter::bind_for`); the field pass has a cache of its own.
- **Groups of passes that are off:** built on their pass's first use, not per frame; a group
  built for a pass that then turns off is dropped 2 encodes later.
- **Dispatches:** `frame/dispatch.rs` holds `Dispatch` (moved from `encode.rs`, now `Copy`) and
  `Dispatches`, a fixed array of `1 + ATROUS_ITERATIONS` per pass; every per-pass `Vec` of
  `encode.rs` and `fsr2/pipelines.rs` is one, and `Fsr2::dispatched` is cleared and refilled in
  place.
- **Light table:** `light_table` keeps its chosen chunks (`LightNear`, keyed on
  `GpuWorld::light_epoch`, the camera chunk, the light radius and the light buffer) and
  re-weighs them by the eye each encode; collect, sort and truncate run only on a key change.
- **Counts** (`a_frame_after_the_second_builds_no_bind_group`, lavapipe, 48 x 32 internal for
  64 x 48 output): totals after each switch's first two frames, groups / views: Off 28/159,
  filter off 30/179, two a-trous iterations 34/211, gradient 40/247, lamp bounce 43/253, lamp
