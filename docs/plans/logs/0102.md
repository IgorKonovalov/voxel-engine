## Phase 2: `own` leaves `svet-render`

**What the commit holds.** The library change and every caller the compiler forced: the
fly-through's source, examples and tests stop naming `UpscalerKind::Own`, `Upscaler`,
`set_jitter`, `set_sharpness`, `set_upscaler` and `upscale_history`, so the workspace builds and
the phase's gates run at this commit. The CLI's refusals, the re-pointed and restated tests and
`gpu_upscale.rs`'s deletion land with it for the same reason; Phase 3's commit holds what is
left of its list (the golden file, its readings).

**Design points the plan left to the code** (recorded in the plan's Notes):

- FSR 2's input pass bound the `own` upscale pass's tuning buffer (`FrameInputs::lamp`,
  `fsr2/input.wgsl` binding 5) for its light-change thresholds, and the encode picked the
  older light list by `Upscaler::change_frames`. They are now constants of the frame at the
  same values, `CHANGE_REL` 0.1, `CHANGE_ABS` 0.01 (exported under `internals`) and
  `CHANGE_FRAMES` 24, in a 16-byte `LightChangeUniform` the frame writes once; the input
  pass's struct is `LightChange { change_rel, change_abs, _pad0, _pad1 }`. The `own.*`
  settings rows go, so the thresholds are no longer a live setting.
- `Off` renders at the output size (question 2, option a). `Frame` keeps the internal size
  `set_sizes`/`with_sizes` asked for (`UpscaleState::requested`) and renders at it only under
  FSR 2, so a frame built at unequal sizes and then given FSR 2 runs at the asked size as
  before. `RenderSettings::clamped` sets the scale to 1 under `Off`, and `apply_settings`
  applies the clamped value: a scale change under `Off` is no change and restarts nothing;
  turning FSR 2 off at a scale other than 1 names `scale` and `upscaler` and replaces the
  targets once.
- `Frame::jitter`, `Frame::sharpness`, `Frame::upscaler`, `Frame::set_jitter`,
  `Frame::set_sharpness`, `Frame::set_upscaler`, `Frame::upscale_history`, `Upscaler`,
  `UPSCALE_WGSL`, `SHARPEN_WGSL` and `internals::JITTER_PHASES` are gone; `FRAME_PASSES` has
  22 entries.
