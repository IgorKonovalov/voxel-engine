## Phase 8: The sun's step and the edit's ring, key-only re-bake jobs

- **Built:** `lighting.key_step` (degrees, `DEFAULT_KEY_STEP` 3, live, a settings-file row; no
  menu row or flag). The queue scans every full, non-coarse block's sun stamp (`KEY_SUN_AT`)
  whenever the key light has turned 0.25 degrees since its last scan (`KEY_SCAN_DEG`); a block
  whose stamp the light has turned past by more than the disc's radius and the step, or one
  stamped 0 (baked with no key light), waits for a key-only job (`queue/keys.rs`). Key-only jobs
  open each step before the digs (which go ahead of them at the loop's front) and ahead of the
  pool's, ordered by the queue's score for a chunk with a block, at most 16 a step
