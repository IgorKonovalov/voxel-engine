## Phase 3: stable instance slots, generations in the hit record, ghost leaves, no scratch

- **The hit record holds 16 flag bits, not 32.** `hit_info` is `rgba16uint` (`hit.wgsl`:
  "bits 6..15 its instance"), so the record's instance field is 10 bits, not the 26 the plan's
  Context names. The record carries the slot (9 bits) and the **generation's low bit**; word
  25 of the instance carries `slot | generation << 9` with the 17-bit generation. A slot's
  generation moves at most once a write (a per-slot guard), so two consecutive frames' records
  of a slot whose occupant changed differ in that bit. `entity_info` masks the field to 10
  bits before the shift (`ENTITY_RECORD_BITS`); `prev_world`, `entity_moved` and
  `entity_shadow_scale` mask the slot (`ENTITY_SLOT_MASK`); `same_thing` is unchanged (it
  compares the whole field). Word 25 held the entity's drawn index, which no shader read.
- **Names, plan to tree:**
  - `EntityInstances` (`entities/instances.rs`): `slots` (generation, the write that last
    bumped it, the frame before's part-to-world, a fresh flag), `RangeAlloc` over 512 slots,
    `runs: BTreeMap<EntityId, Run>`; accessors `run_of`, `generation`, `leaves`, `walks`.
    `EntityMirror::instances()` and `EntityMirror::walks_entities()`.
  - A generation bumps when its slot's run is freed (the entity left, or its part count
    changed) and when compaction moves a run out of or into it, once a write.
  - Compaction packs every live run down from slot 0 in slot order.
  - An entity listed twice in one write is counted over (runs are keyed by id).
  - Ghost leaves: every run freed at this write leaves its last box as a leaf with count 0 and
    `LEAF_MOVED`, while the tree has spare leaves (live + ghosts at most `MAX_ENTITIES`; the
